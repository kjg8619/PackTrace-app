## What this repository contains and what it does not

- **No card images, pack artwork, set logos or other publisher images** are
  included. Card images and set logos are loaded at run time from the TCGdex
  asset host and cached on the user's own Mac. Pack front artwork is optional:
  the registry shipped here is empty and packs use a plain stand-in wrapper.
- **No card catalogues** are tracked in Git. `catalog-manifest.json` lists the
  snapshots the app is built with; they are installed separately with
  `scripts/prepare-catalogs.sh` (see README).
- The opening sounds in `Sources/PackTraceUI/Resources/sounds` are generated by
  `scripts/generate-opening-sounds.py` in this repository.
